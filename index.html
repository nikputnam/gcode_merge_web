Extruder clearance
What do these mean?

Each object is printed from the bed up while the earlier ones stand finished beside it. The same clearance model as TRAvel web decides how close they may be: looking down the nozzle axis, a finished object may stand higher than the nozzle tip by an amount that depends on how far away it is.

  • Closer than the nozzle radius: at most one layer. In practice objects must not overlap.
  • Between nozzle radius and shroud radius: at most the nozzle height. Coasters and tiles can be packed this closely.
  • Beyond the shroud radius: at most the gantry height, or unlimited if blank. One object taller than the gantry can still go last.

Only the part of an object above each threshold counts, so a wide foot under a narrow tower needs less room than the tower. Measure your extruder from the nozzle tip and add a millimetre or two of margin.
